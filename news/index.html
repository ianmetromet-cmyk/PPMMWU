Campaign update

From election to action.

Across our unionized clinics, members are preparing for negotiations and building the organization we need to win a strong first contract.

  1. 1

    Our Bargaining Team is elected.

    Across our unionized clinics, we elected our Bargaining Team—comprised of RN, HSS, and Clinical coworkers—to represent us in negotiations.

  2. 2

    Member Action Teams are forming.

    Our Member Action Teams (MATs) are now forming across clinics to build worker, community, and political support for our first contract.

    Learn more and join the Member Action Team →
  3. 3

    Members will vote on our Bargaining Principles.

    In the coming weeks, workers will vote to authorize the priorities we will bring to the table. Negotiations are right around the corner. Stay tuned!
